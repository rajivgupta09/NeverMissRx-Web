Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: August 15, 2026

NeverMissRx uses a single-account, multi-profile model: one account holder manages their own medication schedule plus profiles for the family members they help track. Deleting your account deletes everyone's data under that account — all family member profiles, medicines, dose history, and scheduled reminders — not just your own login.

Option 1: Delete from within the app

This is the fastest way, and works if you still have the app installed and can log in.

  1. Open NeverMissRx and log in
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Re-enter your password to confirm

Once confirmed, your access is blocked immediately. Your data is then held for a 7-day grace period — logging back in during that window lets you cancel the deletion and restore full access. After 7 days, everything is permanently and irreversibly deleted.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app installed

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't log in, you can request deletion by email instead:

Email nevermissrx@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

We'll verify the request and process the deletion manually, within 7 days of receiving it — the same window as the in-app grace period above.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, login credentials)
  • All family member profiles added under your account, including any photos
  • All medicine schedules and dose history for every profile
  • All scheduled reminders
  • Any feedback you submitted through the in-app feedback form
